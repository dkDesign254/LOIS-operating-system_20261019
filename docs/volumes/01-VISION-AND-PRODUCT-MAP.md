# Volume 1: Vision, scope and the product map (v1.0, 31 October 2026)

## Purpose
LOIS gives decision-makers clarity at one point. It brings what an organisation knows, what the outside world says, and what its tools can do into one place, and it shows a person what is happening, why, how sure anyone is, and what they can do next. LOIS prepares and recommends; a person decides.

## The commercial purpose
LOIS is the MVP through which The Lucrebag sells automation and AI services, starting with distributors and wholesalers: let a business try a real use case in a sandbox with its own information or a template, see it work, then subscribe so limits are raised and live runs are enabled.

## What LOIS is meant to become
One system combining: an AI assistant grounded in approved knowledge and the web; a Knowledge bank with human review; a Use case hub that runs automations through Make, n8n, Zapier, LangChain-style agents and others; an ERP and CRM core (contacts, deals, quotes, invoices, receipts, stock, accounting records) with connectors for Odoo and Zoho; analytics for owners; and teaching of decision frameworks inside every decision.

## Phase 1 is a section
"Phase 1" (LOIS never executes anything) was the first section of the product, not its limit. From KB-023 the rule is: sandbox runs use test data and touch nothing; live runs happen only through a connector an owner or admin has switched on with a logged reason, and each live run waits for approval by a person other than the requester. Autonomous operation per use case is a later, explicit owner decision (Volume 8).

## Principles
1. Clarity first: every action explains what, when, why, how, expected effect, accuracy and options.
2. A person decides; LOIS shows its working.
3. Evidence is labelled (verified, reported, example) and never promoted silently.
4. Free tiers until clients pay.
5. Privacy by design under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and GDPR principles.
6. Nothing is deleted from history; corrections are new versions.
