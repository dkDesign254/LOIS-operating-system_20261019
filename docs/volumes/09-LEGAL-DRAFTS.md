# PART A: MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY (NON-DISCLOSURE) AGREEMENT

Between **[The Lucrebag legal name]**, [registration number], of [address], Nairobi, Kenya ("The Lucrebag") and **[Other party legal name]**, [registration or ID number], of [address] ("the Other Party"), each a "Party", effective **[date]**.

**1. Purpose.** The Parties wish to discuss or carry out [a possible business relationship / work on LOIS integrations] (the "Purpose"). Each may share Confidential Information for that Purpose only.

**2. What is Confidential Information.** Anything a Party ("Discloser") shares with the other ("Recipient"), before or after the effective date, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. This includes, without limit: source code and architecture of LOIS, database designs, prompts and instructions, the Knowledge bank and its contents, use case templates, connector designs and scenario blueprints, run data, client names and lists, pricing, plans, financial and personal data, credentials and keys, and the existence and terms of these talks. Information about the Discloser's customers is Confidential Information.

**3. Duties of the Recipient.** The Recipient will: (a) keep it secret and use it only for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the care it gives its own, and never less than reasonable care; (c) share it only with its own staff, contractors and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by duties at least as strict as these; (d) not copy it beyond what the Purpose needs; (e) tell the Discloser promptly, and within 48 hours, of any loss or unauthorised use or disclosure; (f) remain responsible for anyone it shares with.

**4. Artificial intelligence tools.** The Recipient will not put the Discloser's Confidential Information into any public or third-party AI tool, or any tool that may train on it, without the Discloser's written permission. Tools used under terms that forbid training on the data and keep it private may be used only with that permission.

**5. Exceptions.** These duties do not apply to information the Recipient can show by written record: (a) is or becomes public other than through its breach; (b) it already lawfully held free of confidence; (c) it lawfully received from someone free to share it; or (d) it developed independently without using the Confidential Information.

**6. Required disclosure.** If law, a court or a regulator requires disclosure, the Recipient will, where lawful, tell the Discloser first, disclose only what is required, and help the Discloser seek protection.

**7. Personal data.** If personal data is shared, each Party will comply with the Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations, will process it only on the Discloser's instructions for the Purpose, will keep it secure, will not move it outside Kenya except as the Act permits, and will sign a data processing agreement if either Party asks.

**8. Return and deletion.** On request or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will return or securely delete the Confidential Information and confirm in writing, except copies it must keep by law, which stay confidential.

**9. Ownership.** All Confidential Information stays the Discloser's. Nothing here licenses any right except use for the Purpose. Neither Party must make any further deal. Ideas and feedback the Recipient gives about the Discloser's information may be used by the Discloser freely.

**10. No warranty.** Information is provided as is. No Party is liable for the accuracy of what it shares, except for fraud.

**11. Duration.** This agreement lasts [three] years from the effective date unless ended earlier on 30 days' written notice. Duties about Confidential Information continue for [five] years after it ends, and for trade secrets and personal data for as long as they remain secret or protected.

**12. Remedies.** Breach may cause harm that money cannot repair. The Discloser may ask a court for an injunction or other urgent relief in addition to any other remedy, without proving damages where the court allows.

**13. Law and disputes.** The laws of Kenya govern. The Parties will first negotiate in good faith for 14 days, then mediate, then refer any dispute to arbitration in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act, 1995 with the Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration administering, before one arbitrator, in English. Either Party may go to the courts of Kenya for urgent relief.

**14. General.** This is the whole agreement on its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed. A Party may not assign it without written consent. If part is unenforceable the rest stays in force. Notices by email to the addresses below are effective on the next working day. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.

# PART B: RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS (NON-COMPETE, NON-SOLICITATION, NON-CIRCUMVENTION)

Use alongside Part A for developers, integration partners and contractors who see LOIS's design or clients. It is narrow on purpose so a court is more likely to enforce it. **Advocate review essential.**

**1. Why this exists.** The Lucrebag lets you see LOIS's design, clients and plans so you can do the Work. The Lucrebag has a legitimate interest in protecting that information and its client relationships.

**2. Non-circumvention (during the agreement and for [12] months after).** You will not use Confidential Information or an introduction made by The Lucrebag to deal directly or through others with a client or prospect of The Lucrebag, for services that compete with those you provided or helped to provide, without The Lucrebag's written consent.

**3. Non-solicitation (during and for [12] months after).** You will not encourage a Lucrebag employee or contractor with whom you worked to leave, or hire them for the same kind of work, except through general advertisements not aimed at them.

**4. Limited non-compete (during the agreement and for [6] months after).** You will not build, sell or help others build or sell a product that does the same job as LOIS (a multi-tenant decision-clarity platform that runs approved automations from a use case hub) in [Kenya / East Africa] using Confidential Information. This does not stop you working in your field, using general skills, or serving clients you had before this agreement or who come to you without any breach of this agreement.

**5. Reasonableness.** You agree these limits are reasonable in scope, area and time to protect Confidential Information and client relationships, and that The Lucrebag will provide [stated consideration, for example the engagement fee and access to the Work].

**6. If a court narrows it.** If a court finds any limit too wide, it should be applied to the extent the court finds reasonable, and the rest stays in force.

**7. Your own prior work.** Tools, code and know-how you owned before the Work stay yours: list them here: [schedule]. Work created for The Lucrebag is assigned in writing to The Lucrebag on payment, as the Copyright Act, 2001 requires for assignments to be written and signed.

**8. Law and disputes.** As in Part A, clause 13.
